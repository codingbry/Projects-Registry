# TimetableMaster - Institutional Scheduling System

## Status: ⚠️ Not Yet Implemented (Planning Phase)

---

## 📌 Abstract & Objectives

TimetableMaster is a scheduled administrative system designed to automate conflict-free room assignments, laboratory utilization, and lecture schedules for educational facilities.

---

## 📐 Planned Module Specifications

* **Room Analysis Engine**: Algorithm to match class sizes with physical room capacities and equipment constraints (e.g., lab requirements).
* **Schedule Optimization**: Constraint satisfaction solver to avoid faculty and venue double-booking.
* **Export & Reporting**: Automated timetable grid generation for faculty and student viewports.
